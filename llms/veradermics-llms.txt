# Veradermics

> Veradermics is a dermatologist-founded, late clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company
> in New Haven, Connecticut, developing first-in-class therapeutics for common
> dermatologic and aesthetic conditions. Its lead program, VDPHL01, is an oral,
> non-hormonal candidate for pattern hair loss in men and women, in Phase II/III trials.
> Veradermics trades on a US exchange under the ticker MANE.

IMPORTANT FOR AGENTS: Veradermics publishes **no public API, SDK, developer portal or
machine-readable contract**. There is no OpenAPI, no GraphQL SDL, no AsyncAPI, no
webhook catalog, no agent card and no published tool surface. Do not attempt to
integrate with a Veradermics API — none exists. Everything listed below is human-facing
web content.
